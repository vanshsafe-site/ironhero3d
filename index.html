FLIGHT BRIEFING

How to play Iron Hero 3D

Open the game and choose Deploy Suit. Defend the city from Ultra Bots, defeat Ultronium and survive the next wave. Armor regenerates slowly; energy powers your weapons and boost.

On mobile, use the left joystick to move and drag the right side to aim. Labeled buttons control firing, flight and abilities. Mobile play requires landscape and a browser that supports fullscreen.

MISSION QUESTIONS

Before you deploy

Is Iron Hero 3D free to play?

Yes. Play directly in your browser without a download or account.

Can I play on a phone?

Touch controls are available. Use a WebGL-capable browser with fullscreen support and rotate to landscape. Some mobile browsers do not support the fullscreen requirement.

What if the game runs slowly?

Select Performance in the game’s graphics settings. This reduces render resolution and reflection update frequency.

How do waves and bosses work?

The first wave has 20 Ultra Bots, the second has 40, then 80 and so on. Drones arrive in groups of up to 20. Ultronium appears after you defeat all drones in a wave.
